NASA publishes its data for free. This is what you can build with it.

Nine pages, each reading a public NASA or JPL feed straight into the browser: satellites in orbit, asteroids on approach, eruptions on the Sun, the Earth from deep space. Start with the solar system in 3D or the satellites overhead.

The explorers

  • NEO 3D Planets, near-Earth asteroids and comets on their real orbits, in three dimensions. NeoWs, JPL Horizons, SBDB
  • Satellite 3D The space station and any satellite you search for, propagated live around a day-and-night Earth. TLE API
  • EPIC Earth The whole sunlit face of Earth, photographed daily from 1.5 million km away. DSCOVR EPIC
  • Solar storms Coronal mass ejections: how fast, when they arrive and how far south the aurora could reach. DONKI
  • Earth events Wildfires, storms, volcanoes and ice, mapped as NASA catalogues them. EONET
  • Exoplanets Filter the confirmed planets around other stars and chart mass against radius. Exoplanet Archive
  • Mission images Search NASA’s photo archive from Mercury through Webb. Image and Video Library
  • Mars rovers Rover photography by rover, camera and Martian day. Image and Video Library
